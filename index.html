What shipped this month

This release delivers seven changes to CURE Compass. Two of them change how donations and trip invoices are handled and require action from the Clinics and Colleges team. The remaining five are additive — new data and new reporting, with no change to existing workflows.

Changes requiring action

Two items

Both concern the same workflow: how a donation reaches a participant's trip invoice.

Fundraising Pages in Compass

Action required Clinics & Colleges team, and anyone entering gifts

What changed

GoFundMe Pro fundraising pages now synchronize into Compass and appear under Fundraising Pages in the Clinics app navigation. Pages arrive within approximately one hour of being created and are matched to the corresponding trip participation automatically wherever the participant's email address allows it. No one creates a page by hand, and no one copies a page ID.

Why it changed

Page IDs were previously transcribed by hand from GoFundMe Pro. Crediting therefore depended on accurate manual entry, and funds raised above a participant's trip cost were not recorded anywhere.

What you will see

Each page names itself from its title, creation month, and GoFundMe ID — for example, Sarah's Kenya Page – Aug 2026 (7356034) — so a returning participant's pages remain distinguishable. The page record shows the fundraising goal and progress, the total GoFundMe Pro reports as raised, the total Compass has actually received, the variance between those two figures, any unused surplus, and the full list of donations.

Reading Sync Variance

ZeroThe two systems agree.
PositiveGoFundMe Pro counted money Compass never received — usually a check keyed directly into GoFundMe Pro. Worth investigating.
NegativeCompass holds offline gifts GoFundMe Pro does not know about. Expected.

What you need to do

Two views require a weekly review, and both should normally be empty:

  • Unlinked Pages — pages the synchronization could not match to a participation, generally because the email address differs between the two systems or the contact is duplicated. Identify the participant and enter the page ID on their trip participation record.
  • Gifts Needing Manual Linking — donations whose page ID matched more than one participation. Correct the incorrect page ID; the correction re-triggers processing automatically.

Where a match is ambiguous, Compass declines to guess and parks the record in one of these views. No funds are ever attributed to the wrong participant silently — but that safeguard only works if someone reviews the views.

Automated invoice crediting

Action required Clinics & Colleges team, and anyone entering gifts

What changed

Donations now apply themselves to the participant's trip invoice, capped at the outstanding balance. Any amount above that balance is recorded as surplus against the gift rather than rejected. The invoice is marked Paid automatically once credits equal its total.

Why it changed

A donation larger than the remaining invoice balance previously failed with a business process error and produced no credit at all. Someone had to notice, intervene manually, and the excess remained untracked.

Worked example

A $3,000 donation against an invoice with $2,300 outstanding now produces a $2,300 credit and $700 of recorded surplus. Nothing is lost and nothing requires rescuing.

Late participations

Donations that arrive before a trip participation exists in Compass now wait rather than fail. When the participation is created carrying the page ID — or when an incorrect page ID is corrected — the waiting donations link and credit themselves. There is no queue to work through and no button to press.

Discontinued

  • Setting an invoice on a transaction in order to force an automatic credit. This mechanism has been retired.
  • Manually creating invoice credits for GoFundMe Pro donations.
  • Copying page IDs from GoFundMe Pro for pages the synchronization has already matched.

Unchanged

  • Manual invoice credits for participants who have no fundraising page and are paying by check.
  • Entering offline gifts as transactions, exactly as before.

New: linking an offline gift

Enter the transaction as usual, then select the participant's page in the Fundraising Page lookup. Compass fills the page ID, links the participation and invoice, and credits the gift exactly as it would an online donation. The page ID field beside the lookup is read-only by design: a selection cannot introduce a typo, and a typed number can.

New: reallocating surplus to another trip

To move a participant's unused funds to a subsequent trip, select their previous page in Apply Surplus From Page on the new participation record. Compass transfers the available surplus — never more than the gifts hold, never more than the new invoice requires — then clears the field for reuse. The Over-fundraised Pages view lists everyone with funds available.

Your selection constitutes the approval. Whether donor funds may move between trips is a judgment the system will not make on its own.

Recipient Facilities

No action this release Programs, assessments, logistics

What changed

Compass now holds a Recipient Facilities table containing more than 5,000 hospitals and clinics that Project C.U.R.E. has assessed or shipped to, loaded from the Health & Compassion database. Every facility is searchable as Name (Country), so entering "St. Mary" returns St. Mary's Hospital (Ghana) in any search or lookup.

What a facility record holds

Current facts — bed count, staffing, population served, top requested needs — each carrying a banner stating which assessment booklet they came from and when. Alongside them sits the facility's full history: the files it has been part of, the shipments it has received, and every assessment on a timeline.

What you do differently: nothing, this release

Continue working accounts and connections exactly as you do today. The facility layer is read-only for staff while the data is verified. Facility selection on requests, assessments, and shipments arrives in a later release, with training before anything changes.

You are welcome to browse it now. If you spot a duplicate or an obviously wrong record, that is useful — send it to the CRM contact below.

Power BI: opportunity scoping corrected

No action Leadership and fundraising

The executive dashboard previously scoped every measure to opportunities created within the current fiscal year. An opportunity opened in a prior year and still being actively worked therefore disappeared from view at the fiscal year boundary. The Opportunities list now shows every open opportunity regardless of creation date, alongside everything created in the current fiscal year, and estimated revenue reflects all open asks.

Win rate and won amounts remain scoped to the current fiscal year, so year-over-year comparisons continue to mean what they always have. Currency figures now display unit-aware — thousands as K, millions as M — rather than rendering everything in millions.

GIK request reference numbers

No action GIK and warehouse teams

Every GIK donation request now carries a reference number in the form GDR-001008, generated automatically on creation and read-only on the form. Use it in email, on receipts, and in hand-offs so there is no ambiguity about which request is meant.

One caveat. The approximately 3,300 requests that predated the reference number were numbered in bulk within the GDR-100000 block. A higher number therefore does not indicate a more recent request. Sort views by created date, not by reference number — the number is an identifier, not a chronology.

Training and support

The two action-required changes have a full written guide covering every screen, field, and new habit, plus a short video for each task. Start with the overview video if you handle gifts, and use the guide as a reference afterward.

If something looks wrong

  • A donation has not credited and does not appear in either review view — note the transaction number and the page name, then get in touch. That is enough to trace it.
  • A page's figures look wrong — check Sync Variance first. A positive variance almost always means a check was keyed directly into GoFundMe Pro.
  • You applied surplus and the totals have not moved — open the gift itself. Its Surplus Remaining updates immediately, while page summary totals refresh on a slower cycle.
